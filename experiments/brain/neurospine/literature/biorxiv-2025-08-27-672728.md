# Learning Local Geometry and Nonlinear Topology of Neural Manifolds via Spike-Timing Dependent Plasticity

## Mechanism (from abstract)

The authors use topological-data-analysis methods to quantitatively track how nonlinear (non-flat) neural manifolds, specifically circular and toroidal coordinate systems seen in orientation-selective V1 cells, head-direction cells, and grid cells, propagate across a population during learning. They ask whether nonlinear coordinate structure is generic to network architecture or must be learned, and show computationally that in a range of feed-forward connectivity/parameter regimes, learning is required, and that simple Hebbian spike-timing-dependent plasticity (STDP) reorganizes such networks to correctly propagate circular coordinate systems. During this learning process, the model spontaneously produces receptive-field types matching experimental observations: bimodally-tuned head-direction-like cells and spatially periodic, band-like receptive fields, offering a mechanistic, falsifiable account (a specific plasticity rule) for the emergence of nonlinear neural-manifold geometry.

## Provisional relevance

Provisional: touches tribe-neuroprint because manifold geometry (linear vs. circular/toroidal) is directly relevant to how PerceptionDecoder and DecisionDecoder should model latent state spaces; assuming a linear/Euclidean latent space where the true manifold is toroidal would silently degrade decoding.
Provisional: touches decision-phenotype because head-direction/grid-cell-like periodic codes are canonical examples of state representations relevant to sequential decision tasks (AIM-DDM's state space).
Provisional: touches wiring-not-weights because STDP is a wiring-level (synaptic weight change) mechanism proposed as necessary and sufficient for a specific computational property (manifold topology), giving a concrete, falsifiable instance of the weights-produce-function argument to test against the wiring-matters alternative.
Provisional: informs gate G6 (mechanism and necessity) strongly because the paper both proposes a mechanism (STDP) and demonstrates it is necessary in the tested connectivity regimes (without learning, the correct topology does not propagate), the exact form G6 requires: a falsifiable mechanistic story plus an intervention (ablating plasticity) that removes the effect.
Provisional: supports NEUROSPINE aim A1 by supplying a manifold-geometry-aware feature-space choice (circular/toroidal vs. linear) that could materially change individual-scale decoder accuracy and replicability if real neural data has nonlinear manifold structure the current linear decoders miss.
