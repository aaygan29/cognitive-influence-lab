# Bayesian Computation through Cortical Latent Dynamics

## Mechanism (from PubMed abstract)

According to PubMed, Sohn et al. record monkey frontal cortex during
a time-interval reproduction task and find that prior statistics
warp the neural representation, allowing sensory-to-motor mapping to
implement Bayesian inference. Recurrent neural network models trained
on the task reproduce the effect and reveal that the warping is
enabled by a low-dimensional curved manifold. Causal RNN perturbations
implicate the curved-manifold structure as the substrate of Bayesian
integration.
