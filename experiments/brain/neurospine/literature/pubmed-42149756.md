# EffortNet: A Deep Learning Framework for Objective Assessment of Speech Enhancement Technologies Using EEG-Based Alpha Oscillations

## Mechanism (from PubMed abstract)

According to PubMed, Sung et al. present EffortNet, a deep-learning
framework for decoding listening effort at the individual level from
64-channel EEG during speech comprehension. Data: 122 participants,
four conditions (clean, noisy, MMSE-enhanced, Transformer-enhanced).
Alpha power (8-13 Hz) was significantly higher during noisy speech,
confirming its role as an objective biomarker of listening effort.
EffortNet integrates three learning paradigms to handle
inter-individual variability: self-supervised learning on unlabeled
data, incremental learning for per-subject adaptation, and transfer
learning for new subjects. Result: 80.9 percent classification with
only 40 percent of new-subject training data, beating conventional
CNN (62.3 percent) and STAnet (61.1 percent). Neural probability
metric aligns with objective metrics but dissociates from subjective
intelligibility ratings.

Provisional: informs G7 (calibration via probability-based output);
