# The computational and neural substrates of individual differences in impulsivity under loss framework

## Mechanism (from PubMed abstract)

According to PubMed, Jiang et al. combine hierarchical Bayesian
drift-diffusion modeling with inter-subject representational
similarity analysis (IS-RSA) to link individual impulsivity (K)
to computational (DDM drift rate) and neural (prefrontal + OFC
+ precuneus activation and gray-matter volume) substrates across two
experiments (total n = 155). Drift rate mediates brain to behavior
relations in Exp. 1; IS-RSA shows that inter-subject variation in
activation patterns is mediated by drift-rate variation. Replicated
in Exp. 2 (n = 58).
