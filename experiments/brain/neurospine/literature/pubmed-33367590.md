# Decoding Music-Evoked Emotions in the Auditory and Motor Cortex

## Mechanism (from PubMed abstract)

According to PubMed, Putkinen et al. scanned 102 subjects listening
to instrumental music evoking happy, sad, fearful, and tender
emotions, then again while watching short emotional film clips. GLM
analysis and multivariate pattern analysis (MVPA) revealed that
music-induced categorical emotions are decodable from auditory,
motor, and interoceptive cortices, and largely NOT from the limbic
and medial prefrontal regions that film-clip emotions engaged. The
authors interpret this as music-induced emotions relying on
sensorimotor and interoceptive representations rather than the
survival-value emotional circuitry.

## Provisional relevance

Provisional: touches affectprint, behavioral-decoding, memoryprint
because it provides a stimulus-modality contrast (music vs film)
that is directly usable as the G4 specificity ablation for any
AffectDecoder trained on video-elicited affect.
Provisional: informs G4 (specificity ablation via music vs film),
G6 (mechanism / necessity), G8 (external validity across stimulus
modalities).
Provisional: supports NEUROSPINE aim A1 by giving an AffectDecoder
external anchor that names precisely which cortical regions carry
categorical emotion information and which do not.
