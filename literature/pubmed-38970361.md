# Empathy incites a stable prosocial decision bias

## Mechanism (from PubMed abstract)

According to PubMed, Saulin et al. use a binary social decision task
under fMRI, with participants exposed to frequent painful stimulation
of another person in block one, and low frequency in block two.
Drift-diffusion modeling revealed an increased initial bias toward
prosocial decisions in block one that persisted into block two. A
behavioral replication confirms the finding. A reciprocity-driven
control condition did NOT show the same stability, so the effect is
specifically empathy-mediated. Dorsomedial prefrontal cortex
activation tracked empathic concern under frequent pain and a general
prosocial bias under rare pain.
