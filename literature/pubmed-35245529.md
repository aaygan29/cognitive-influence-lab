# Anxiety and amygdala connectivity during movie-watching

## Mechanism (from PubMed abstract)

According to PubMed, Kirk, Robinson, Skipper test whether the
amygdala-prefrontal connectivity story from constrained face-viewing
paradigms replicates in movie-fMRI naturalistic settings. Result:
anxiety-dependent face-dependent superior parietal activation and
speech-dependent auditory cortex activation are found, BUT
anxiety-dependent amygdala-prefrontal connectivity is NOT found in
two independent samples, either stimulus-dependent or -independent.
Interpretation: findings from tightly-controlled tasks may not
replicate in ecologically-valid movie-fMRI. Direct call for
generalizability testing outside the original context.

## Provisional relevance

Provisional: touches affectprint, cortex-of-anyone,
behavioral-decoding because any AffectDecoder or emotion-relevant
decoder trained on constrained-task fMRI must be tested on
naturalistic movie fMRI before its A2 (group-scale) claim is
credible.
Provisional: informs G4 (specificity ablation: constrained-task vs
naturalistic-task contrast is the natural G4 for any affect-related
decoder), G8 (external validity: a decoder that passes on
constrained tasks may fail on naturalistic ones and vice versa),
G12 (analytic integrity: report the exact task context in which any
decoder was trained).
Provisional: supports NEUROSPINE aim A2 (transfer must be tested
across task contexts, not only across subjects) and A3 (declared
frontier: some cognitive constructs may only appear under specific
task designs).
