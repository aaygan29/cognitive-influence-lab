# Optimal Transport Features for Morphometric Population Analysis

## Mechanism (from PubMed abstract)

According to PubMed, Gerber et al. augment neuroimaging morphometric
analysis with an unbalanced-optimal-transport feature extraction
step. Motivation: for pathologies with spatially dispersed tissue
loss, standard voxelwise statistics are underpowered and confounded
by spatial misalignment. Unbalanced OT separates volume-difference
changes from location changes and reduces sensitivity to
misalignment. Demonstrated on OASIS-1 (Alzheimer's).

## Provisional relevance

Provisional: touches memoryprint, behavioral-decoding,
cortex-of-anyone because cross-subject alignment on the manifold of
tissue distributions is precisely the A2 (group-scale transfer)
substrate NEUROSPINE will need for BOLD Moments and NSD subject
transfer.
Provisional: informs G4 (specificity via decomposing volume vs
location), G5 (confound control against misalignment), G8 (external
validity across subjects with different brains), G14 (manifold
correctness: unbalanced OT distances are metrics on the space of
non-negative measures; a candidate for a "distribution manifold"
family in `LatentState` on future ADR).
Provisional: supports NEUROSPINE aim A2 by adding
Wasserstein-distance alignment as an alternative to RAVEN weak-to-
strong when the subject-to-subject transfer is best framed as a
distributional matching problem rather than a linear-subspace shift.
