# The Artists' Brain: A Data Fusion Approach to Characterize the Neural Bases of Professional Visual Artists

## Mechanism (from abstract)

The authors apply joint independent component analysis (jICA), a multimodal data-fusion method, to structural (gray-matter volume, white-matter fractional anisotropy) and functional (resting-state regional homogeneity, ReHo) MRI data from 12 professional visual artists and 12 matched controls, to identify a coordinated, cross-modality neural signature distinguishing expertise-driven brain reorganization. A single joint component (IC2) significantly separates artists from controls, reflecting coordinated increases in gray matter (parietal, temporal, frontal, posterior cingulate), white-matter integrity (thalamic radiations, corticospinal tract, association fibers), and functional homogeneity (basal ganglia, cerebellum). IC2 expression correlates with self-reported visual imagery vividness, linking the neural signature to a specific cognitive ability. The authors explicitly caveat that the sample (n=12 per group) is small and replication with larger samples is necessary.

## Provisional relevance

Provisional: touches affectprint and cortex-of-anyone because data-fusion (jICA) across structural and functional modalities is a candidate method for detecting individual, domain-specific expertise or trait signatures relevant to idiographic modeling, and visual imagery vividness is a directly measurable individual-difference covariate.
Provisional: touches wiring-not-weights because the identified signature spans both structural (wiring-like: gray matter, white matter integrity) and functional (activity-like: ReHo) measures simultaneously, offering a template for jointly modeling wiring and function rather than treating them as separate axes.
Provisional: informs gate G2 (seed variance) negatively: with n=12 per group and no seed/resampling variance reported, this result does not meet NEUROSPINE's own bar and should be treated as a hypothesis-generating pilot, exactly as the authors themselves caveat.
Provisional: informs gate G8 (external validity) as an open item: no independent replication cohort is reported, so this is a clear "external validity missing, risk quantified as high given small n" case for anyone citing it.
Provisional: supports NEUROSPINE aim A3 (declared unmeasured cognitive domains) because visual imagery vividness and domain-specific expertise are plausible components of the cognitive-dark-matter space not currently covered by NEUROSPINE's named decoders (Perception/Affect/Decision/Memory/Reward), worth explicitly declaring as unmeasured unless a VisualImageryDecoder or equivalent is added.
