# Inferring Macroscale Brain Dynamics via Fusion of Simultaneous EEG-fMRI

## Mechanism (from PubMed abstract)

According to PubMed, Philiastides, Tu, Sajda review methods for
fusing simultaneous EEG-fMRI recordings, with machine learning as
the central toolkit. They dichotomize methods into symmetric (both
modalities inform fusion equally) and asymmetric (one modality
constrains the other). The review argues that fusion yields brain-
function insights not accessible from either modality alone. Future
directions named: mesoscale mapping at ultrahigh magnetic field,
targeted perturbation-based neuroimaging, and deep learning to
uncover nonlinear representations linking electrophysiological and
hemodynamic measurements.

## Provisional relevance

Provisional: touches ism-v1 (closed-loop self-model would benefit
from concurrent EEG-fMRI grounding), behavioral-decoding (multi-
modal fusion is the study's core competence), cortex-of-anyone
(mesoscale mapping is exactly the individuation target).
Provisional: informs G1 (provenance: fusion output must trace back
to both source modalities); G6 (mechanism: asymmetric-fusion methods
are candidates for the brain_state estimator in ADR-007); G8
(external validity: fusion insights that survive across sites and
subjects are the ones that anchor NEUROSPINE); G9 (measurement
reliability: fusion changes what "reliable" means, needs re-testing
per modality contribution).
Provisional: supports NEUROSPINE aim A1 by giving a review-level
reference for the symmetric-vs-asymmetric fusion decision that ADR-
007's `brain_state` estimator will face on adoption; A3 (declared
unmeasured: fusion may reveal signals that a single modality misses
and change what NEUROSPINE explicitly does not measure).
