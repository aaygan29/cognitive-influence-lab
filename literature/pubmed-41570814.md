# High-dimensional structure underlying individual differences in naturalistic visual experience

## Mechanism (from PubMed abstract)

According to PubMed, Han and Bonner use spectral decomposition of
fMRI responses during naturalistic movie viewing across the visual
cortical hierarchy. Result: idiosyncratic neural patterns persist
across multiple orders of magnitude of latent dimensions, and each
dimensional range encodes qualitatively distinct aspects of
individual processing. The multidimensional neural geometry
predicts subsequent behavioral differences in memory recall,
including variation in the concreteness versus abstractness of
narrative descriptions. These fine-grained inter-individual
variability patterns cannot be reduced to conventional intersubject
correlation measures. Framing: subjective visual experience arises
from information integrated across an expansive multidimensional
manifold.
