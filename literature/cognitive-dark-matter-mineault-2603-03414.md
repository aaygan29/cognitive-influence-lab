# Cognitive Dark Matter: Measuring What AI Misses

## Mechanism (from abstract)

The paper proposes that AI capability gaps stem from cognitive dark matter (CDM): brain functions influencing behavior yet difficult to extract from observable actions alone. Six key CDM domains are identified: metacognition, cognitive flexibility, lifelong learning, abductive reasoning, social and common-sense reasoning, and emotional intelligence. Current AI evaluation metrics largely absent these functions. The authors outline a research strategy using three complementary data sources: latent variables from cognitive models, process-tracing methods (eye-tracking), and paired neural-behavioral datasets. Training AI systems on cognitive processes rather than outcomes alone is predicted to yield more robust and versatile intelligence while deepening human cognition understanding.
