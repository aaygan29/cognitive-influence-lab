# Learning brain dynamics for decoding and predicting individual differences

## Mechanism (from PubMed abstract)

According to PubMed, Misra et al. apply an RNN architecture to fMRI
movie-watching data to learn distributed spatiotemporal signatures.
They report ~90 percent accuracy on 15-way movie-clip classification
at region-level and ~60 percent binary experimental-condition
classification at voxel-level. The model also predicts individual
differences in fluid intelligence and verbal IQ at levels comparable
to existing techniques. Dimensionality reduction over the RNN latent
uncovers low-dimensional trajectories that preserve classification-
relevant properties of brain dynamics. Saliency maps and lesion
analysis characterize brain-region/voxel importance. At voxel level,
the framework is a dynamic version of multivariate pattern analysis.
