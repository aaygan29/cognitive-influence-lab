# Sophisticated Inference

## Mechanism (from PubMed abstract)

According to PubMed, Friston, Da Costa, Hafner, Hesp, Parr present
"sophisticated" active inference: a recursive expected free-energy
functional in which agents hold beliefs about the counterfactual
consequences of actions for latent states, not just for states per se.
The recursion implements a deep tree search over sequences of belief
states rather than over states. Special cases include reinforcement
learning, Bayes-optimal inference, and Bayes-optimal design.

## Provisional relevance

Provisional: touches ism-v1 (closed-loop self-model as a shallow
active-inference agent), cultist (belief-imparting is a sophisticated
inference on an audience's belief states), warden (calibrated
abstention is a special case of the expected-free-energy minimization),
decision-phenotype (DDM plus prior warping is a special case of
active inference under a specific generative model).
Provisional: informs G6 (mechanism: expected free energy provides a
principled objective that any NEUROSPINE intervention channel should
be scored against for consistency), G12 (analytic integrity: the
math is unambiguous, so implementations either match it or explicitly
deviate), G15 (purpose gate: "sophisticated" beliefs about
counterfactual consequences of an intervention IS the purpose-scoring
problem NEUROSPINE's ADR-009 registry will formalize).
Provisional: supports NEUROSPINE aim A4 (intervention scoring) as the
formal-mathematical anchor for the "score channels by expected effect
under a purpose" architecture.
