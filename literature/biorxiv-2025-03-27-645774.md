# The Reward Positivity Does Not Encode Current Reward Value

## Mechanism (from abstract)

The reward positivity (RewP), a frontocentral EEG event-related potential elicited by reward feedback, has been widely interpreted in the literature as reflecting reward value and motivational state. The authors test this directly using reinforcer devaluation: hungry participants (n=31) performed a modified Doors Task with Pavlovian conditioning to sweet and savory food reinforcers matched in pleasantness, then underwent selective satiation (devaluation) of one reinforcer between two task rounds. Behaviorally, devaluation worked as intended: participants rated the devalued food less pleasant and adjusted win-stay/lose-switch choices in a sensory-specific way, confirming the manipulation changed current reward value. Despite this, permutation testing (tmax statistic) found no significant difference in RewP amplitude associated with reinforcer devaluation, meaning the RewP did not track the change in reward value even though behavior clearly did.

## Provisional relevance

Provisional: touches decision-phenotype and tribe-neuroprint's RewardDecoder component directly, and is the clearest invalidating result found in this scan: if RewardDecoder or any NEUROSPINE component treats RewP amplitude as a proxy for current, state-dependent reward value, this paper is a direct empirical counter-example using a well-controlled devaluation design with confirmed behavioral sensitivity.
Provisional: informs gate G4 (specificity ablation) because the devaluation manipulation is itself a specificity control: it isolates value-tracking from generic feedback processing, and shows RewP tracks something other than current value (the specific mechanism claimed does not hold under the matched control).
Provisional: informs gate G5 (confound control) because it demonstrates that a standard, widely-used EEG marker can be dissociated from the psychological construct (reward value) it is commonly assumed to index, a caution for any RewardDecoder design that inherits assumptions from the ERP literature without re-testing them.
Provisional: supports NEUROSPINE aim A1 negatively: it is a reminder that individual-scale, replicable prediction claims must be checked against well-controlled null results in the base literature, not just built on the assumption that a canonical marker measures what it is named for.
